# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

**the rolv project** ("we", "our", or "us") operates the rolv desktop application and website. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect when you use rolv and how you control it.

## 1. Local-First Architecture

rolv processes your project files, scripts, and analytical data locally on your computer. When you use a local LLM (such as Ollama), your prompts and results stay on your device.

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Account Information
When you create an account or sign in (via email or Google Sign-In using Firebase Authentication), we store your email address and account identifier to authenticate you, manage cloud recipes, and enforce free-tier usage quotas.

### B. Usage Metrics and Diagnostics
- **Active Usage Duration:** When you are signed in, rolv records aggregated active app usage duration (total and monthly hours) for your account so we can understand engagement and improve the app. This metric does not include file names, prompts, scripts, or analytical data. You can turn it off at any time in **Settings → Execution Tools → Usage Telemetry**.
- **Cloud Token Counts:** When you use rolv's cloud AI gateway, we record token counts to enforce daily and monthly usage limits.
- **Improvement Reports (Optional):** When repeated workflow or environment failures occur, rolv can prepare a redacted diagnostic report. You can review, edit, snooze, or disable these reports in **Settings → Execution Tools → rolv Improvement Reports**. File paths, email addresses, and secrets are stripped automatically before sending.

### C. Cloud AI Processing
When you use rolv's cloud AI gateway, the prompts and excerpts required to answer your request are sent to our servers and upstream AI model providers solely to return your result. We do not use your prompts or analytical data to train AI models.

## 3. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain account and usage records only as long as needed to provide the service. To request deletion of your account and associated data, email [hello@rolv.io](mailto:hello@rolv.io).

## 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post any updates on this page with a revised date.

## 5. Contact Us

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact us at [hello@rolv.io](mailto:hello@rolv.io).
