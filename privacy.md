# Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2026

**rolv technologies LLC** ("we", "our", or "us") operates the rolv desktop application and website. This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

## 1. Local-First Approach

rolv is designed with a **privacy-first architecture**. By default, your data, analytical workflows, and queries are processed locally on your machine when using local AI models. We do not have access to data processed locally.

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Account Information
If you create an account or subscribe to our Pro/Pro+ tiers, we collect information such as your email address and authentication tokens to manage your subscription and cloud credits.

### B. Usage Data
We may collect diagnostic data such as crash reports and minimal usage metrics (e.g., app version, OS type) to improve the stability of our application. This data is anonymized and does not include your personal files or analytical data.

### C. Cloud Processing Data
If you explicitly opt-in to use our cloud-based optimized LLMs or cloud compute features, the specific queries or data subsets you send to the cloud will be processed by our servers. This data is strictly used to return the requested analysis and is not stored permanently or used to train our models without your explicit consent.

## 3. Third-Party Services

We use **Paddle** as our Merchant of Record to handle subscriptions and billing. We do not store or collect your payment card details. That information is provided directly to Paddle whose use of your personal information is governed by their Privacy Policy.

## 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
